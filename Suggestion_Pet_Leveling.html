01 THE SYSTEM

A simple gameplay loop

Pet levels add long-term progression to a system already based on hatching, equipping, and fusion—without replacing the game's core airplane-throw mechanic.

STEP 01

Equip a pet

Only currently equipped pets earn leveling XP. Each pet keeps its own level and progress.

STEP 02

Throw airplanes

Each server-validated throw grants active XP to every equipped pet.

STEP 03

Earn passive XP

While equipped and in-game, each pet also gets +1 XP approximately every 5 minutes.

STEP 04

Grow stronger

Each completed level increases that pet's own stat contribution. Cosmetic milestones mark progress.

✦ PET POWER SCALING

Each completed level: +2% to that pet's own stats

Level 1 = baseline · Level 50 = +98%

Formula: Final Pet Bonus = Base Pet Bonus × [1 + 0.02 × (Level − 1)]. At Lv. 23, the bonus is +44%, not +46%—because Level 1 is the starting level and does not count as a completed upgrade. This multiplier affects the pet contribution, not the entire player's stats.

05 LONG-TERM PROGRESSION

Fusion should protect your effort

Leveling adds emotional and gameplay value to pets. Fusion must preserve the invested progress rather than make players regret training a pet before evolving it.

Keep the highest trained pet

When several pets are fused, use the ingredient with the highest level/progress as the leveling base. Show the chosen base in a fusion preview.

Preserve level + progress %

The result retains the base pet's level and its percentage toward the next level. Apply the new rarity/fusion XP requirement only to future progress.

Make consumed XP visible

Other ingredients are consumed. At launch, their XP is not added automatically—show a clear warning to prevent accidental loss of trained pets.

Example

A Golden pet at Lv. 18, 60% becomes a Giant at Lv. 18, 60% if it is the chosen base. The next-level XP target is recalculated.

A possible later upgrade is a small, capped transfer of XP from consumed pets. Do not simply sum all ingredient XP: that can create leveling exploits and make fusion disproportionately powerful.

07 GAMEPLAY INTEGRITY

Rules for a clean implementation

Small decisions here prevent bugs, AFK abuse, and frustrating inventory behavior.

XP applies to equipped pets

Every valid airplane throw grants XP to every equipped pet, without dividing the reward among the team. Unequipped pets gain nothing.

Online passive time only

Each equipped pet earns +1 XP for every five minutes of eligible in-game time. Time spent unequipped does not accumulate. No offline XP.

Server-validated throws

Only successfully processed throws count for XP. The server should enforce normal throw cooldowns and prevent duplicate client events. Legitimate Auto Throw can count.

Equip Best uses final stats

Sorting and Equip Best must account for pet fusion and level multipliers, not just the base pet's rarity or original bonus.

Protect trained pets

Every pet is a unique inventory item with its own level and XP. Show warnings before deleting or fusing trained pets, especially Lv. 25+ or Lv. 50 pets.

Persist reliably

Store the pet's unique ID, species, rarity, fusion tier, level, current XP and equipped state. Recalculate XP requirements server-side after a fusion.

✦ BALANCING NOTE

At 20 valid throws/minute and 2 XP per throw, active progression earns approximately 40 XP/minute per equipped pet. The passive rate adds just 0.2 XP/minute (around 0.5% of active income), so it is intentionally a very small background reward—not a meaningful AFK leveling strategy. The final XP curve should be calibrated using real throw frequency and the intended hours required to reach Lv. 50.
