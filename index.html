The game behind the ideas

What is +1 Plane Throw?

+1 Plane Throw is a Roblox simulator about launching paper planes farther and farther. Train your Power, earn Wins from your throws, collect pets, unlock better planes and explore new worlds.

That simple loop is what makes the game fun to build on—and what inspires this little library of community ideas.
